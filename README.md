# Personalized Meal & Diet Subscription Manager

## Problem Statement #19 – Healthcare & Telemedicine

A clinical dietary subscription system that generates weekly meal plans based on a subscriber's dietary requirements such as allergies, calorie goals, and other metabolic constraints. It also manages meal-plan approval and daily delivery tracking. fileciteturn0file1L3-L8

## Main Actors

- **Subscriber** – Manages their dietary profile, subscription, and deliveries.
- **Dietitian** – Reviews and approves generated meal plans.
- **Payment Gateway** – Processes subscription payments.
- **Notification Service** – Sends delivery and meal-plan notifications. fileciteturn0file0L150-L169

## Key Features

- Generate personalized weekly meal plans
- Validate allergen and macro-nutrient constraints
- Dietitian review and approval
- Pause, skip, or reschedule deliveries
- Track daily delivery status
- Process subscription payments
- Send notifications
